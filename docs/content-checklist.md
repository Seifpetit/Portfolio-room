# Seif's Room: what I need from you

One template per section, so every item looks the same. Send it as a message or a file, in any order. Anything you skip stays as a placeholder.

## 1. Projects (tech lab screen)

Every project uses the same card and the same detail view. Two to six projects works best. The screen scrolls sideways past that, but the best ones should come first.

**Per project, the card (what shows on the screen):**
- [ ] **Name**, 1 to 3 words (e.g. "Pixel Planner")
- [ ] **Thumbnail**, one image, 16:9, 960×540 or larger. PNG or JPG. Pixel art, a screenshot or a cover all work, but use the same style across projects.
- [ ] **One-line description**, 60 characters or fewer (what it does and who it is for)
- [ ] **Tags**, 2 to 3 (e.g. JS, Canvas, API)
- [ ] **Badge** (optional): "Featured", "New" or none
- [ ] **Main button**: label ("Live demo" or "Repo") and its URL

**Per project, the detail view (what opens when someone clicks the card):**
- [ ] **Gallery**: 2 to 4 screenshots (16:9, same size as the thumbnail). Or one short video, MP4 under 10 MB or a YouTube/Vimeo link.
- [ ] **What it is**, 2 to 3 sentences
- [ ] **Your role**, one line (e.g. "Solo: design, code, art")
- [ ] **Stack**, the full list of tools
- [ ] **Highlight**, one sentence on the hardest or proudest part
- [ ] **Links**: live demo URL and/or repo URL (at least one)

**Per project, the capybara line:**
- [ ] **What Seif says when the card is hovered**, one sentence, ideally under 90 characters (e.g. "This one took three weekends and a lot of coffee.")

**Once, for the whole section:**
- [ ] **Intro line** for the tech lab. Right now it reads: "The tech lab, this is where the experiments grow... Let me pull up the projects." Keep it or send a new one.
- [ ] **Order** of the projects, best first

## 2. CV (bureau paper)

One vertical paper, one page.

- [ ] **Photo**: square, at least 400×400. A pixel-art version of you or the capybara works too.
- [ ] **Name** and **role/title**, one line (e.g. "Seif Jlassi, Front-end developer")
- [ ] **Short summary**, 2 to 3 sentences
- [ ] **Experience**: for each, company, role, dates, and 1 to 2 bullets (max 3 entries so it fits the page)
- [ ] **Education**: school, degree, years
- [ ] **Skills**: 6 to 10 items, grouped if you like (Languages, Tools, Design)
- [ ] **Languages spoken** (optional)
- [ ] **PDF download**: the real CV as a PDF. A "Download CV" button goes on the paper.
- [ ] **Capybara line** shown when he opens the CV, one sentence. Right now: "My bureau. Every paper here is part of the story... Here is the CV."
